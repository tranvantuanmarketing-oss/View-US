## Institutions
- Health / end of life: médecin traitant, urgences, réanimation, EHPAD, unité de soins palliatifs, HAD, Sécu, mutuelle, carte Vitale, aide-soignante.
- Legal / notary / inheritance: notaire (mandatory: succession, immobilier, testament authentique), réserve héréditaire (children CANNOT be
  disinherited), donation, assurance-vie (the workaround), indivision, avocat, tribunal judiciaire, huissier.
- Marriage / divorce / custody: mairie (civil marriage is mandatory, before any church), PACS, contrat de mariage / communauté réduite aux acquêts,
  divorce par consentement mutuel via lawyers, JAF, garde alternée, pension alimentaire, "la prestation compensatoire".
- Banking / insurance / benefits: Crédit Agricole, La Banque Postale, Caisse d'Épargne, Livret A, prêt immobilier, CAF, allocations,
  RSA, France Travail, retraite, "le chéquier".
- Police / courts: gendarmerie (rural), commissariat (city), porter plainte, main courante, ordonnance de protection, tribunal.

## Rituals
- Wedding: mairie (the maire with the écharpe tricolore, "témoins"), église optional, vin d'honneur (wide guest list), repas (narrow guest list — being invited only to the vin
  d'honneur is an insult), pièce montée, "la robe", EVJF.
- Funeral: pompes funèbres, cérémonie à l'église or crématorium, "présenter ses condoléances", faire-part, "ni fleurs ni couronnes",
  meal after the service, Toussaint bringing chrysanthèmes to the grave.
- Inheritance: the notaire summons each person, no group reading of the will; "les droits de succession"; children cannot be cut out — the plot must go through a donation, assurance-vie, or "la maison au nom de".
- Family occasions: réveillon de Noël 24 (huîtres, foie gras, bûche; who makes the bûche), jour de l'An, Pâques (agneau), fête des Mères,
  "le repas du dimanche chez belle-maman", 14 juillet, Toussaint, galette des rois (fève).

## Translation tells (≥ 10)
1. Wrong vous/tu: belle-mère and bru usually use "tu"; "vous" between mother-in-law and daughter-in-law exists in bourgeois families = DELIBERATE coldness; notaire, médecin: vous.
2. "$" → €; "2.3 million" → 2,3 millions; "1,200" → 1 200; space BEFORE : ; ! ? (« Tu viens ? »).
3. English-style "…" quotes → « … » with inner spaces; dialogue in books uses the tiret –; FB accepts both.
4. "Mom/Dad" → maman/papa; "mère" is cold; "belle-maman" is sarcastic; the belle-mère is called by prénom or "Madame" (cold).
5. "Attorney/lawyer" for a succession → notaire; avocat for divorce.
6. Church wedding first → the mairie is mandatory and is "the wedding day"; "on s'est mariés à la mairie de Roubaix".
7. Thanksgiving, dinde, casserole → réveillon de Noël, huîtres, foie gras, bûche; "le repas du dimanche".
8. Date "December 7" → 7 décembre (month in LOWER case); time "3 p.m." → 15 h / "trois heures".
9. Dialogue too standard → "bah", "du coup", "genre", "franchement", "t'as vu", "ouais", drop the "ne" ("c'est pas", "j'sais pas").
10. "Mrs. Martin" → Madame Martin; within the family, prénom.
11. "Hospital" for everything → hôpital / clinique / EHPAD / urgences / soins palliatifs are different places.
12. Miles, pounds, Fahrenheit → km, kilos, degrés.
13. "Nursing home" → EHPAD (the everyday word); French "hospice" means something else (the old poorhouse).
14. "Sheriff" → gendarmes / la police; "the judge" → le juge, le JAF.
15. Anglo names (Kevin, Jordan, Brandon, Dylan) = working-class names born 1990-2000 that get mocked; use deliberately for class.
16. Plot "disinherited" → impossible in France (réserve héréditaire); must go through a donation, assurance-vie, "mis au nom de".
17. Number "2.5" → 2,5; "1.200" → 1 200; "€" AFTER the number with a space.
