## Institutions
- Health / end of life: NHS (free — the drama is the waiting list, not the bill), GP, A&E, ICU, hospice, Macmillan nurse, care home,
  district nurse, "111", "the consultant".
- Legal / notary / inheritance: solicitor (wills, property), probate, will, executor, lasting power of attorney (LPA), Land Registry,
  inheritance tax (40% above £325k — a plot point), barrister only in court.
- Marriage / divorce / custody: register office, registrar, church wedding, prenup (limited legal force), no-fault divorce (20 weeks), family court,
  child arrangements (do not say "custody"), child maintenance (CMS).
- Banking / insurance / benefits: Barclays, NatWest, Halifax, current account, overdraft, ISA, state pension, Universal Credit,
  child benefit, housing benefit, mortgage, "repossession", the DWP brown envelope.
- Police / courts: police, 999, PCSO, non-molestation order, magistrates' court, crown court, bailiffs.

## Rituals
- Wedding: register office or church, "the reception", "the wedding breakfast" (the main meal), best man speech, hen do / stag do,
  evening guests (invited only for the evening = an insult), buffet.
- Funeral: funeral director, crematorium (the majority), "the wake" at the pub or village hall, sandwiches and sausage rolls, order of service,
  "donations in lieu of flowers", "the curtains closed" at the crematorium.
- Inheritance: solicitor's letter, probate; no reading of the will; "the house has to be sold to pay the tax".
- Family occasions: Christmas dinner 25th (turkey, pigs in blankets, crackers, paper hats, the King's speech), Boxing Day, Mother's Day (March),
  Sunday roast at mum's, Bonfire Night, "Christmas at ours or yours" is a yearly war.

## Translation tells (≥ 10)
1. "attorney", "mom", "apartment", "parking lot", "cell phone", "trash", "vacation", "fall" → solicitor, mum, flat, car park, mobile, bin, holiday, autumn.
2. "$", "dollars" → £, pounds, quid; "$2.3 million" → £2.3 million.
3. American spelling: color, realize, center, gotten → colour, realise, centre, got.
4. "Hospital bill", "insurance denied the claim" → the NHS is free; UK drama is "the GP couldn't fit her in", "six months on the waiting list".
5. "Courthouse wedding", "county clerk" → register office, registrar.
6. "Nursing home" → care home; "ER" → A&E; "the doctor" → the GP; "the nurse" ok.
7. "Custody battle", "child support" → child arrangements, maintenance.
8. Thanksgiving, prom, high school, sophomore → Christmas dinner, the leavers' do, secondary school / sixth form, "the school run".
9. Dialogue too American: "y'all", "awesome", "I guess" → "innit", "proper", "knackered", "I'm not being funny but", "bless", "love/pet/duck" by region.
10. "Mr. Smith" → Mr Smith (no full stop); "Mrs Smith" for strangers; mother-in-law called by name or "your mum".
11. Keep miles (the UK uses miles); body weight → stone/pounds; temperature → Celsius.
12. "Cookies", "candy", "fries", "chips" → biscuits, sweets, chips, crisps.
13. Class markers must be consistent: "tea" (evening meal, the North) vs "dinner"; "lounge/front room/living room" — pick one by region and keep it.
14. The rich talking in figures → the UK hides money; it shows through private school, "the house in Cornwall", "the Aga", "his mother's people".
15. "£2.3 million inheritance" is very rich; 40% inheritance tax is a plot point UK readers grasp instantly — use it.
16. "Sweetie/honey" from the mother-in-law → "love", "dear", "darling" (bourgeois); "sweetheart" ok.
17. Date "12/7" → 7 December or 7/12; "December 7th" acceptable.
