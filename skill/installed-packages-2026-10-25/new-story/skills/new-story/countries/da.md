## Rituals
- Wedding: rådhus or kirke, taler, toastmaster, brudevals BEFORE 24:00 (guests cheer), "klippe strømper/slips", sang written by guests to a known tune, kagen.
- Funeral: bisættelse (cremation) or begravelse, mindesammenkomst med kaffe og boller/smørrebrød, dødsannonce, kirkegården, bedemand, "kisten".

## Translation tells (≥ 10)
1. "De/Dem" → not used; "du" with everyone, including doctors, lawyers, bosses, mother-in-law.
2. "$" → kr. / kroner; "2.3 million" → 2,3 millioner; "1,200" → 1.200 kr.
3. Wrong value scale: 2,3 mio is a flat, not a fortune; rich = 15-30 mio; "arv på 23 millioner".
4. "Mom/Dad" → mor/far (also in direct address: "Mor, kom nu"); mormor/farmor/morfar/farfar distinguish the side; svigermor is called by name.
5. Church wedding as default → rådhus is very common; many couples are "papirløse" with 2 children — normal.
6. Instant divorce → Familieretshuset, separation 6 mdr or straight away if both agree; "7/7-ordning".
7. "Reading of the will" → skifteretten; uskiftet bo; tvangsarv.
8. Date "December 7" → 7. december (lower case); time "3 p.m." → kl. 15 / "klokken tre".
9. Quotes "…" → straight quotes are fine on FB; books use »…« or „…“.
10. Dialogue too standard → "altså", "jo", "ik'?", "øh", "nå", "sgu" (mild), "helt ærligt", "nej hvor hyggeligt" (sarcastic).
11. Thanksgiving, turkey → juleaften flæskesteg/and, risalamande, mandelgaven.
12. "Nursing home" → plejehjem; "hospice" ok; "ER" → skadestuen/akutmodtagelsen; "the doctor" → egen læge.
13. "Mr. Nielsen" → first names, even for bosses and doctors.
14. "Notary" → not used for everyday wills; an advokat does it; "notartestamente" at byretten is a rare word.
15. Flaunting money openly → janteloven: wealth shows through sommerhus i Skagen, privatskole, Arne Jacobsen-stole, "Hellerup-mor".
16. "Hygge" overused, with an explanation → Danes use "hyggeligt" once, naturally, without explaining.
17. Number "1,200" → 1.200; "2.5" → 2,5; "kr." AFTER the number.
