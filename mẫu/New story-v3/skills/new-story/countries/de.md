- Poor: Minijob 538 Euro, Bürgergeld, Warmmiete 680 Euro, Nachzahlung Nebenkosten 400 Euro, "das Konto war im Minus", Pfandflaschen, Tafel.

## Institutions
- Health / end of life: Hospiz, Pflegeheim, Intensivstation, Hausarzt, Krankenkasse (AOK, TK), Pflegegrad 3, Palliativstation, Notaufnahme.
- Legal / notary / inheritance: Notar (required for notarised wills, buying a home), Erbschein, Nachlassgericht, Testament (handschriftlich is valid), Pflichtteil, Grundbuch.
- Marriage / divorce / custody: Standesamt (civil marriage is mandatory), Trauzeuge, Scheidung, Familiengericht, Sorgerecht, Unterhalt, Jugendamt, Trennungsjahr (1 year of separation before divorce).
- Banking / insurance / benefits: Sparkasse, Volksbank, Girokonto, Dispo, Riester, Rente, Bürgergeld, Kindergeld, Elterngeld, Wohngeld.
- Police / courts: Polizei, Anzeige erstatten, Amtsgericht, Landgericht, Gerichtsvollzieher.

## Rituals
- Wedding: Standesamt first (everyday clothes, 20 minutes), kirchliche Trauung afterwards if any, Polterabend, Hochzeitszeitung.
- Funeral: Trauerfeier, Beerdigung/Urnenbeisetzung, Leichenschmaus/Kaffeetrinken after the service, Traueranzeige in the newspaper, Bestatter, Friedhofsgebühr.
- Inheritance: no group "reading of the will" — the Nachlassgericht sends a letter (Testamentseröffnung); the Erbengemeinschaft fights through an Anwalt.
- Family occasions: Weihnachten (Heiligabend 24.12 is the main evening), Kaffee und Kuchen on Sunday, Ostern, Konfirmation/Kommunion, Geburtstag — whoever hosts pays.

## Translation tells (≥ 10)
1. Wrong Sie/du: mother-in-law and daughter-in-law use du; lawyers, doctors, police use Sie. Using "Sie" with the daughter-in-law is a DELIBERATE cold detail, not the default.
2. English-style quotes "…" → „…“ (opening low, closing high). Quotes within quotes ‚…‘.
3. "Dollar", "$" slipping in → Euro; decimals use a comma (2,3), thousands a dot (1.200).
4. American names (Jessica, Tyler) for German characters over 40 → see the name table; "Kevin/Justin/Mandy" are class-mocking names, use deliberately.
5. Date "December 7" / "7 December" → 7. Dezember, or "am siebten"; time "3 p.m." → 15 Uhr / "um drei".
6. "Anwalt" for everything → buying a home and wills go through a Notar; for divorce an Anwalt is mandatory for at least 1 party.
7. Church wedding as the main event → the Standesamt is mandatory and usually the real "wedding day"; church is optional.
8. "Reading the will" in front of the whole family → does not exist; a letter from the Nachlassgericht or a Notar opens the Testament.
9. "Mom/Dad" → Mama/Papa (working-class), Mutter/Vater (formal, cold); "Schwiegermutter" is usually called by name or "seine Mutter".
10. Machine-translated sentences: "Ich konnte nicht anders, als…", "Wenig ahnte ich…", "Es war in diesem Moment…" → see `anti-ai-patterns.md` section de.
11. Divorce right after proof of an affair → must go through the Trennungsjahr; characters know this ("ein Jahr getrennt leben, dann erst").
12. Dialogue too standard Hochdeutsch → real people say "halt", "eh", "krass", "ne?", drop the subject ("Hab ich nicht gesehen").
13. American digits "1,200 Euro" → 1.200 Euro; "Millionen" capitalised, plural "Euro" unchanged.
14. "Krankenhaus" for a hospice → Hospiz / Palliativstation; "Pflegeheim" is not "Krankenhaus".
15. "Friedhof" for the funeral service → Trauerfeier (service) / Beerdigung (burial) / Bestatter (funeral home).
